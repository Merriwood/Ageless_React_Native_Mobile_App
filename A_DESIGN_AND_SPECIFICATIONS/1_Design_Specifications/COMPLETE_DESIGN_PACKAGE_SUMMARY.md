## Complete Workflow: From Now to Production

### Phase 0: PWA Screenshot Matching (1-2 weeks) ⭐ NEW
**When:** As you forward PWA screenshots
**Duration:** 1-2 hours per screen (9-12 hours total)
**Output:** PWA_DESIGN_MATCHING_REPORT.md + Updated specifications

1. **Receive PWA screenshot** (you forward to us)
2. **Analyze screenshot** using PWA_SCREENSHOT_MATCHING_GUIDE.md
3. **Extract visual details** (colors, typography, spacing)
4. **Compare to plan** specifications
5. **Document differences** in report
6. **Update specifications** if needed
7. **Get team sign-off**
